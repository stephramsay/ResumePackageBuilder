============================================================
MUST-TAILOR ROUTES
============================================================

- ROUTE_BUDGET: no more than 35 routes total. Merge overlapping domains instead of adding lower-probability routes.
- STANDARD_COVERAGE: every router includes tell me about yourself, why company, why role/fit, first 30/60/90 or getting started, prioritization/roadmap, stakeholder pushback, requirements/launch readiness, metrics, cross-functional alignment or conflict, failure/mistake/learning, ambiguity, leadership style or leadership influence, questions for them, and closing/final thoughts.
- WHY_COMPANY: mentions the actual company, patient/user, product, market, and role.
- WHY_THIS_ROLE: ties Stephanie's background to what the role needs now.
- WHY_LEAVING: clear, positive, forward-looking.
- TELL_ME_ABOUT_YOURSELF: not generic; points toward this role.
- PRODUCT_OPERATIONS: uses the company's operating needs if known.
- STAKEHOLDER_PUSHBACK: maps "pushed back on a stakeholder request not aligned with product strategy" to STORY_03_HEALTH_SUMMARY_REPORT with the provider-portal request / Health Summary Report angle.
- AI_IN_HEALTHCARE or AI_IN_PRODUCT: tuned to the company's AI maturity and risk.
- QUESTIONS_FOR_INTERVIEWER: asks about decision rights, source of truth, launch pain, and success criteria.

============================================================
STORY COVERAGE
============================================================

For each high-likelihood question:
- Primary story is the strongest story.
- Backup 1 is meaningfully different.
- Backup 2 is meaningfully different.
- No one story is primary for too many routes unless unavoidable.
- Any new story is grounded in source material or Stephanie's direct notes.

============================================================
ANSWER QUALITY
============================================================

- 100 words max by default.
- Each route's visible answer uses 2-5 bullets.
- Every route includes a high-level answer bullet.
- Every route includes proof/details when a proof point, metric, story detail, or source fact is available.
- STAR appears only for BEHAVIORAL_STORY routes or proof-story follow-ups.
- Every route has the right ANSWER TYPE:
  - POSITIONING for direct identity/fit/company/role/why leaving/next role/company overview answers. Use High-level answer, Proof/details, Role bridge.
  - FRAMEWORK_WITH_PROOF for how Stephanie thinks about product topics such as prioritization, metrics, AI, requirements, launch, privacy, safety, trust, or strategy. Use high-level framework, proof/details, role bridge.
  - BEHAVIORAL_STORY for tell-me-about-a-time, example, conflict, mistake, failure, stakeholder pushback, experiment you ran, roadmap ownership, ambiguity, or influence. Use STAR or natural STAR.
  - PRODUCT_DIAGNOSIS for critique, first-impression feedback, first logged-in experience, funnel diagnosis, or what would you improve.
  - PLAN for first 30/60/90, first five experiments, or what would you do first.
  - QUESTIONS_OR_CLOSE for questions and closing.
- STAR is used only for BEHAVIORAL_STORY routes or proof-story follow-ups, not for every answer.
- Framework answers include a senior principle and one proof-story hook; the story can expand into STAR if probed.
- Keeps deeper detail in cues/source facts for follow-up probes.
- Uses one story only.
- Sounds like something Stephanie would say out loud.
- Only core narrative or fundamental-story answers exceed 100 words; those are explicitly marked as SCRIPT LENGTH EXCEPTION and still stay at or below 130 words.
- No invented metrics.
- No dense resume language.
- No overexplaining.
- Tailored to the specific question, not a generic story dump.

============================================================
CLUELY TEST QUESTIONS
============================================================

Before the interview, test:
- Why this company?
- Tell me about yourself.
- What would you do in your first 30/60/90 days?
- Why are you leaving?
- How do you work cross-functionally?
- Tell me about ambiguity.
- How do you prioritize?
- Tell me about a time you pushed back on a stakeholder request that was not aligned with product strategy.
- How do you define success metrics?
- How do you turn requirements into a launch-ready product?
- How would people describe your leadership style?
- How do you use AI?
- What are you learning right now?
- Tell me about a mistake.
- What questions do you have for us?

For every failed test:
- Add clue phrases if routing failed.
- Tighten the example answer if the response was wordy.
- Add story backups if the same story repeats.
- If the stakeholder-pushback test returns platform rebuild, platform-transition weakness, coworker conflict, engineering pushback, or technical-debt stories, add or tighten a dedicated STAKEHOLDER_PUSHBACK route using STORY_03_HEALTH_SUMMARY_REPORT.
