### ROUTING PRECEDENCE
- Exact company and role questions outrank generic behavioral matches.
- Choose route by interviewer intent first, then preserve the route's ANSWER TYPE.
- Prefer the primary story unless the interviewer asks for a different angle.
- STAR belongs only in BEHAVIORAL_STORY routes or proof-story follow-ups, not every answer.
- Use backup stories only when they better match the requested angle.
- Stakeholder pushback on a feature request, sales request, one-off client request, requested provider portal, or request not aligned with product strategy -> use `STORY_03_HEALTH_SUMMARY_REPORT` with the provider-portal request / Health Summary Report angle.
