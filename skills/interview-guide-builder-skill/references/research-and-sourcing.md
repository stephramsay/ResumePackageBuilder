# Research and Sourcing Rules

Always browse for current company research before writing the guide.

Prioritize:

- Company website, product pages, blog, documentation, press releases, and careers page.
- Investor, funding, or acquisition announcements from primary sources when available.
- Reliable business or healthcare news for recent strategy shifts.
- LinkedIn/company leadership only when it helps explain org structure or role context.

In `company-research.md`, separate facts from inference:

- `Known facts`: sourced and linked.
- `Interview implications`: reasoned hypotheses based on the facts.
- `Risks or watchouts`: areas to avoid overstating.
- `Questions to ask them`: targeted, company-specific questions.

Do not let company research override Stephanie's approved facts. Use research to tailor framing, not to invent experience.
